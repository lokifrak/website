Far out on the edge of the Milky Way, people come to the frontier to disappear.

Jax Tanner did. He traded his rifle for a welding torch and a quiet life raising his adopted son, Eilean.

It should have stayed quiet.

In the canyon markets, miners are whispering about friends who walked into the deep canyons and found something called "The Silence" — a place with no hunger, no pain.

Jax says it's just a cult.

But Eilean has heard a faint, unexplainable Hum his whole life. And when a stranger brushes past him in the crowd, that Hum suddenly becomes deafening.

That night, Jax's scanner picks up a light where nothing should be lit: a long-abandoned mine.

Something is awake in the canyon. And it just heard Eilean hear it.
